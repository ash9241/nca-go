# Decisions and deviations

- Use Flax linen with JAX; its explicit parameter trees work with Optax, EMA, and checkpoint serialization.
- Use Python 3.11 in a project virtual environment; the system default is Python 3.14.
- Use the supplied position-independent convolutional NCA adaptation; the paper's architecture is described in Section 3 and Appendix B, rather than Section 2.
- Smoke validates plumbing with reduced data and training budgets. It does not certify the research accuracy gates; full runs enforce G0 and G1.
- G0 smoke uses a small seeded cross-check. The separate full G0 requires 10,000 games distributed evenly across 9 and 19.
- Structured positions are legal setup positions with zero-liberty chains removed; they are not claimed to be sampled legal game histories.
- Ladder search budget exhaustion and repeated ko states are masked as unknown, never mislabeled as an escape.
- Full runs require a GPU and a successful recorded smoke run; this prevents accidentally starting a large CPU job.
- Optional binary setup uses project-local binaries or existing executables. No package-manager modification of the user's global environment is performed.
- Smoke uses 16 channels, two perception heads, 80 optimizer steps, 16-step training chunks, and an EMA decay of 0.9 so tiny training can exercise checkpoint selection.
- Smoke evaluates D=32,64 and a capped D_auto of 128; full uses the uncapped information-distance bound and the full schedule through 8192.
- Smoke deliberately continues the functional pipeline after a failed G1 and prominently reports the failed gate; full blocks later phases. Smoke measurements cannot establish research capability or satisfy G1.
- Baseline parameter matching selects the nearest integral width and logs the actual ratio, rather than claiming exact equality.
- Benson smoke uses varied small eyes and near-misses. Full generation balances the three labels at chain level using exact Benson labels, extra eyes and connected boundary growth; its class counts are logged.
- A smoke split check caught synthetic train/test duplicates. Generation now deduplicates and separates splits across every dihedral transform and color swap; surrounding clutter and eye shapes increase synthetic diversity.
- Rendering PCA uses an explicit tensor contraction to avoid spurious Accelerate BLAS floating-point warnings on this Mac; all saved states are checked for finiteness before rendering.
- P-game has no available self-play engine on this host; the initial task datasets explicitly record synthetic and P-random sources.
- Report F10 selects the earliest measured checkpoint reaching 95% of the board's final accuracy, using direct synchronized timings at that checkpoint.
- Full ladder splits pair captured and escaping canonical defenders within principal-variation length bins; other chains on those boards retain their exact labels and are not themselves class balanced. Smoke keeps the small unbalanced split so its poor applicable-chain accuracy is visible.
- Benson opponent-playout properties use a logged horizon of 30 opponent moves per playout; the report states the observed counts rather than claiming exhaustive adversarial validation.
- The optional player advances replay samples to separately teacher-labeled children, uses dihedral augmentation, and omits color-swap augmentation because fixed Black ownership and komi labels would require a new teacher query.
- The optional PUCT implementation evaluates leaves serially; batched search and multi-seed player training remain unvalidated stretch work. The local unit tests exercise heads, gradients, legal search and terminal rollout equivalence, while teacher distillation and match strength are explicitly not run.
- Player inference stores terminal outputs in evaluation batches; warm-start trajectory measurements use a configured subset to bound memory at large rollout depth.
- At initial implementation, the available Mac had only a JAX CPU backend; full-profile training attempts were blocked by the hardware guard and recorded as not run. Subsequent A100 liberty diagnostics are recorded separately and do not relabel those earlier attempts.
- F3 adds separate Benson-cost and ladder-length panels, and F5c plots both accuracy and per-step firing counts to make censored convergence and repair behavior explicit.
- Full structured liberty data explicitly surround the main chain while leaving a sampled count of one to four legal liberties; smoke retains its smaller original generator. Failed legal constructions are retried rather than silently assigned an easier liberty class.
- Add a separate liberty learnability diagnostic in response to failed smoke learning: balance target chains, average loss per chain, compare all-step versus late-window MSE/CE, then test replay and augmentation. These are constructed target-chain probes, not whole-board G1 measurements; final evaluation boards stay sealed.
- The learnability pilot uses final optimizer weights without EMA or checkpoint selection, and reports every longer-rollout result including degradation; broader data, replay, augmentation and initialization changes are a combined recipe comparison, not an isolated causal ablation.
- For controlled locality comparisons, use distance to an answer-changing input in the grid rather than assuming chain diameter is a strict causal bound: the NCA can also communicate through empty and opponent cells.
- GPU investigation adds larger batch sizes and widths, a short-chain curriculum, delayed replay and optional state normalization; screening uses only 9/13 training and validation splits.
- Fix state normalization's undefined gradient at an all-zero mutable state by putting epsilon inside the square root; a regression test checks finite gradients. Previous unnormalized results are unaffected.
- The dedicated A100 cloud worker has a four-hour maximum runtime and no attached service account; source revision is bundled for provenance when the remote checkout has no Git directory.
- A100 screening reaches 99% held-out target-chain accuracy at the trained horizon but degrades at longer horizons and larger validation boards. The next diagnostic sweep tests cosine learning-rate decay, final EMA weights, randomized training horizons, and an optional gated convex update. Gating is an explicit architectural deviation from the paper adaptation; defaults retain the residual NCA. No exact labels or global features are supplied at inference.
- A separate multiscale diagnostic mixes only 5x5 through 9x9 training boards, placed at random offsets in a 9x9 canvas with frozen off-board cells. This tests size and boundary shortcuts without adding 13x13 training data. Full 9x9 and 13x13 validation boards remain separate; sealed final boards are untouched during recipe selection.
- Full recurrent GPU training exposed another normalization failure: epsilon-smoothed unit-sphere projection has a 10,000-fold zero-state Jacobian, which overflows across 32 steps despite finite one-step gradients. Replace this optional operation with unit-ball projection (identity below norm one), and extend the regression to a 32-step gradient. Earlier measurements retain their failed status and source version; this changes only configurations with normalization enabled.
- After a target-chain recipe exceeds 99.5% on held-out 9x9 chains, add a distinct whole-board diagnostic. It uses 60% random legal playout boards and 40% controlled structures, supervises every stone, and averages per-class losses within each minibatch. Its exact metric is whole-board exactness, and validation accuracy is not relabeled as the sealed G1 test.
- Paired distant-liberty queries share all input cells except one point farther than the four-block CNN's radius nine. Couple NCA initial noise and firing masks within each pair so stochastic differences cannot mimic a distant signal. Use only 13x13 validation pairs during investigation; larger paired tests require a frozen checkpoint-hash manifest. This is a specific propagation test, not full Go rule certification.
- An algorithm-hint experiment adds an auxiliary five-class readout for the number of distinct liberties discovered so far (zero through four). A vectorized exact k-smallest-ID teacher generates intermediate labels using the same firing masks; its trajectory is tested against the existing reference CA. IDs and teacher states are never neural inputs and are absent from inference. This is an explicit training-supervision deviation; the matched whole-board recipe differs only by the auxiliary head and loss.
- Batch diagnostic inference to 128 boards to bound GPU memory during concurrent recipes and width comparisons. Fresh zero-noise synchronous predictions are unchanged. Asynchronous evaluation uses separately recorded chunk-key streams; its three trial results remain reported. Training batches, losses and data are unchanged by this inference batching change.
- Random horizons originally placed a GPU conditional around every neural update and were prohibitively slow. Replace the conditional with fixed execution, mask losses after the sampled horizon, reset dummy work to the detached incoming state, and separately return the exact sampled-horizon state for replay. Validate against the earlier two-update checkpoint before restarting the affected case; interrupted runs retain their actual status and are excluded from completed results.
- High multiscale target-chain accuracy failed the distant-query validation under different surrounding clutter. Add an explicit Go graph prior as a separate diagnostic: mutable messages travel only between orthogonally adjacent stones of the same color, while static input perception stays local. This differs from the original grid NCA. Exact labels and liberty IDs remain absent from inference. Mix varied random connected chains with the existing templates, and additionally deduplicate observable component neighborhoods across train/validation splits, ignoring translation and irrelevant distant clutter. Tests verify exact targets, disjoint component keys, and invariance to inputs outside the component's local neighborhoods.
- Run unchanged whole-board recipe repetitions from fresh training seeds, without warm-starting or selecting a favorable checkpoint. Freeze final EMA checkpoint hashes, primary inference depth, test counts and all evaluation depths before opening independent final boards. Exclude all previously opened local and cloud datasets under rotations, reflections and color swap; use a new deterministic final seed namespace. Evaluate different recipes in separate final runs so identical seed numbers cannot pool scores or overwrite predictions.
- Report the numeric G1 threshold separately from the original stronger asynchronous-generalization hypothesis: synchronous recurrent-cell models can pass the accuracy threshold without establishing that hypothesis. Final reports include class-balanced accuracy, all-stone accuracy, board exactness, training-seed standard deviation and a stratified board bootstrap interval conditional on the recorded seeds and firing trials.
- Additional A100 capacity was eventually available in us-central1-c after earlier stockouts. Use a separate two-hour worker for matched graph-message variants, with no attached service account and automatic stop. Preserve worker-specific logs and environments separately; both task-owned workers are cleaned up after their artifacts are downloaded and verified.
- The preselected asynchronous graph model is additionally compared with an otherwise matched no-hint model to separate training fit from distant propagation. Freeze all three graph checkpoints and the 64-step primary depth before opening the larger paired family, and report every depth, including deterioration. Each board size uses one witness geometry varied by symmetry and clutter; this is a locality demonstration, not broad shape generalization. At 37x37 its changed cell is 34 grid steps away, beyond the supplied 16-block ResNet's radius 33.
- Several experiments share one A100 and some are paused to prioritize independent repetitions and final evaluation. Logged training and run wall times include such pauses and contention; they are elapsed-time provenance, not isolated throughput benchmarks.

- The public report embeds all 24 current animations, retains all GPU attempts and historical smoke results, and supplies local Twitter drafts and H.264 videos. The distant animation replays sealed pair zero and firing trial zero with frozen weights and coupled keys; its remaining error is shown. Public metadata uses artifact identifiers instead of machine account paths.
